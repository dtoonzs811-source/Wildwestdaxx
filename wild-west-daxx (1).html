A free open-world horse-riding prototype

Building the frontier…

  • 🕹 Left thumb – walk / steer. Push to the edge for full speed
  • 👆 Right thumb – drag to look around
  • 🐎 MOUNT – hop on when you're next to your horse
  • 💨 GALLOP – latch top speed · JUMP – hop fences
  • 📯 WHISTLE – your horse comes to you
  • 🔫 FIRE – shoots at the red lock-on ring (or where the camera looks). Hits knock people out
  • 🧍 PICK UP a knocked-out person, DROP them, or LOOT their pockets
  • 🏠 Your cabin (blue dot on the map): walk to the bed at night and tap SLEEP
  • ⚖ 3–4 Rulemen (police, with badges) patrol the town and break up fights (orange dot on map)
  • ⚠ Shoot someone and he shoots back — you become WANTED and every Rulemen hunts you. Escape or sleep it off
  • 🕐 Tap the clock to skip 3 hours (see the sunset & night)

Landscape works best. Desktop: WASD move · arrows/mouse-drag look · E mount · Space jump · Shift gallop · Q whistle · F fire · G loot · R reload · T time
